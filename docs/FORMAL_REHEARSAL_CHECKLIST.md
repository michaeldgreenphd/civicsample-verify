# Formal release rehearsal

Checklist identifier: `mu84-formal-rehearsal-1`.

The investigator attests to these checks against a registered synthetic pilot export:

- The reviewed application build and its database migrations have passed their required checks.
- The intended hosted path has been exercised with separate authenticated accounts, including
  reviewer isolation, adjudicator timing, investigator permissions and account locking.
- Each pilot reviewer can save and revise their own answers, finish a complete set, and then
  cannot alter it. A stale finish confirmation is refused.
- Disagreements and single/double Cannot tell cases reach adjudication at the authorized point.
- The reference is locked and its export passes offline integrity verification.
- Mobile and desktop review, guidance, error handling and final submission are usable.
- A private backup and recovery procedure has been checked for the deployment.

Practice data remain excluded from study results. This checklist does not approve scientific
gates, freeze a sample, contact clinicians or authorize formal opening. A checked item needs
observed evidence; unavailable or untested checks are not passes.
