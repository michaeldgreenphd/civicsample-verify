# How to review a trial’s classification

Guide revision 4 · 25 October 2026

Each reviewer assesses the same 100 trial-to-category assignments from 100 different trials: 56 therapeutic-area assignments, 40 condition-group assignments and four checks of whether a trial was correctly included or excluded. Practice and teaching examples do not count toward the 100.

## 1. Read, decide and save

1. Read the trial title and summary, then the other registry information needed to understand what the trial studies. Missing information is shown as missing.
2. Read **Our classification**. This is the classification used in the study, not an answer you are expected to accept.
3. Choose **Agree**, **Disagree** or **Cannot tell**. If you disagree, select the best reason and add a short explanation.
4. Click **Save answer** and wait for the saved confirmation. Selecting an answer alone does not save it. Check **Your answer on record** before moving on.

| Answer          | When to use it                                                                                                                                                                |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Agree**       | The displayed classification fits what the trial studies. If the classification says “excluded,” Agree means that exclusion is appropriate.                                   |
| **Disagree**    | The displayed classification does not fit. Choose a reason and explain it briefly. If the classification says “excluded,” Disagree means the trial should have been included. |
| **Cannot tell** | The registry information lacks something you need to decide. Add a note identifying what is missing.                                                                          |
| **Uncertain**   | You can answer, but are not sure. If the information is adequate and the judgment is difficult, use Agree or Disagree and check Uncertain.                                    |

You can use **Save revision** to change your answer until review closes. The original and later answers are retained. Work independently and do not discuss individual assignments with the other reviewer. You will not see the other reviewer’s answers or AI assessments.

Judge the trial shown. Its registry text may mention another trial or registry; that does not change which trial you are assessing.

## 2. Different things you are judging

Read the classification and its basis before judging it:

- **Our classification:** where the trial was placed, or whether it was included or excluded.
- **Why it was classified this way:** the registry-heading criterion that produced the existing assignment.
- **Your question:** whether that assignment fits the trial’s clinical subject.

The explanation describes the existing sorting. It does not dictate your clinical answer. Your response records agreement or disagreement; it does not reclassify the manuscript’s trials.

| Kind of review                   | What you judge                                                                                     |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Therapeutic area**             | Does the trial study a condition that clinically fits the displayed area?                          |
| **Condition group**              | Does the trial study the displayed condition, using the definitions below?                         |
| **Inclusion or exclusion check** | Was it appropriate to include or exclude this trial from the Diabetes mellitus or Neoplasms group? |

Answer only the displayed question. A therapeutic-area judgment does not also decide the condition group. The four inclusion/exclusion checks become available after the other 96 assignments.

## 3. Therapeutic areas

The existing sorting uses the parent branches of the MeSH medical-subject hierarchy. Mental health also includes the Depression heading under a stated study exception. The ten areas are:

| Area                                        | MeSH parent branch used for the original assignment |
| ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Oncology                                    | Neoplasms                                           |
| Cardiovascular                              | Cardiovascular Diseases                             |
| Mental health                               | Mental Disorders, plus the Depression heading       |
| Infectious                                  | Infections                                          |
| Metabolic                                   | Nutritional and Metabolic Diseases                  |
| Neurological                                | Nervous System Diseases                             |
| Respiratory                                 | Respiratory Tract Diseases                          |
| Pathological conditions, signs and symptoms | Pathological Conditions, Signs and Symptoms         |
| Immune                                      | Immune System Diseases                              |
| Urogenital                                  | Urogenital Diseases                                 |

**Use the displayed MeSH parent heading for all ten areas.** Urogenital, Immune, and Pathological conditions, signs and symptoms follow their MeSH parent headings in the same way as the other areas. No additional narrower clinical definitions are imposed on those three areas.

Use the registry evidence to judge whether the displayed area is appropriate for what the trial studies. MeSH placement explains how the existing assignment was generated; it does not require you to agree. If the placement is clinically misleading, you can disagree and explain why. Apply the separately stated cancer, Depression and smoking-cessation policies below.

### Cancer and additional areas

A cancer trial fits Oncology. An additional area does **not** fit solely because it describes the cancer’s organ or cells of origin, spread, residual disease or course. An additional area **does** fit when the trial explicitly investigates a separate clinical indication in that area.

- A prostate-cancer treatment trial does not also fit Urogenital solely because the prostate is a urogenital organ. A trial treating urinary incontinence after prostate-cancer surgery can fit Urogenital.
- A lymphoma treatment trial does not also fit Immune solely because the cancer starts in immune cells. A trial preventing graft-versus-host disease can fit Immune.
- A metastatic-cancer treatment trial does not also fit Pathological conditions solely because metastasis describes its spread. A trial treating cancer pain or fatigue can fit that area.

Use **“Cancer origin, spread or course only”** when this explains your disagreement. This exception concerns cancers; do not automatically apply it to every benign tumor. Judge benign tumors by clinical fit.

### Depression and smoking cessation

Use the **same Depression definition** for the Depression condition group and the Mental-health area: a depressive disorder, or depressive symptoms that the trial treats, prevents, uses to select participants or measures as its primary outcome. Major depressive disorder is not required. A mention only as an exclusion criterion, safety measure or secondary outcome is insufficient.

Where the Mental-health assignment comes only from the Depression heading, the app explains: “The trial’s MeSH heading Depression is counted as Mental health by the study rule; MeSH files it under Behavior.” Assess it using the same definition.

For **smoking cessation**, Mental health fits when the trial studies tobacco or nicotine dependence, addiction or a clinically defined substance-use disorder. General cessation programmes, prevention programmes or policy interventions do not automatically qualify. “Excluded by the review policy” is the relevant disagreement reason when dependence is not the condition studied.

## 4. Condition groups

These are the ten condition groups behind Figure 2. Each is assessed separately from therapeutic-area membership.

| Group                                            | What supports Agree                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Neoplasms**                                    | A neoplasm of any behavior is present or targeted: benign, borderline, uncertain behavior, in situ or malignant, primary or metastatic. This includes prevention, screening, diagnosis or treatment of a neoplasm. Agreement does not confirm malignancy. |
| **Diabetes mellitus**                            | The trial studies type 2 diabetes, or diabetes whose type the registry does not state. Unstated type is not confirmed type 2. A trial of only type 1, gestational or another specified type, or prediabetes only, does not meet this definition.          |
| **Depression**                                   | A depressive disorder or depressive symptoms meeting the definition above. A major depressive disorder diagnosis is not required.                                                                                                                         |
| **Obesity**                                      | Obesity is a condition the trial studies.                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Asthma**                                       | Asthma is a condition the trial studies.                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD)** | COPD is a condition the trial studies.                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **COVID-19**                                     | COVID-19 is a condition the trial studies.                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Schizophrenia**                                | Schizophrenia is a condition the trial studies.                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Hypertension**                                 | Systemic arterial hypertension is a condition the trial studies. Pulmonary, ocular, intracranial or portal hypertension alone does not qualify.                                                                                                           |
| **Pain**                                         | Pain of any kind or duration is a condition the trial studies, in any population. Acute, postoperative, procedural and chronic pain can all qualify.                                                                                                      |

For Neoplasms, a paraneoplastic or autoimmune condition or inherited predisposition alone does not establish that a neoplasm is studied. Assess what the registry describes; do not assume every heading placed under Neoplasms is clinically a neoplasm.

Confirming any condition group validates **membership as defined here**, not the prevalence benchmark used in the paper. In particular, broad Pain membership does not validate a chronic-pain benchmark, and an unstated diabetes type does not become type 2.

## 5. Check whether inclusion or exclusion was appropriate

These four checks examine decisions made by the study’s diabetes and neoplasm inclusion criteria. The displayed inclusion or exclusion is the decision you are assessing.

### Diabetes inclusion check

The group is intended to retain type 2 diabetes and diabetes of unstated type. The original sorting used registry headings:

| Displayed classification                                               | Why the original sorting produced it                                                         | What you judge                                                                                                                                |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Included in the Diabetes mellitus group**                            | A heading indicates type 2 diabetes, or indicates diabetes without specifying its type.      | Does the registry describe type 2 diabetes, or diabetes whose type it does not state?                                                         |
| **Excluded from the Diabetes mellitus group — another specified type** | The headings indicate type 1, gestational or another specified type, with no type 2 heading. | Is exclusion appropriate based on what the trial studies? Disagree if it studies type 2 diabetes or diabetes whose type cannot be determined. |
| **Excluded from the Diabetes mellitus group — prediabetes only**       | The headings indicate prediabetes only, with no diabetes mellitus heading.                   | Does the trial study only prediabetes? Disagree if it studies diabetes.                                                                       |

On an **included** trial, Agree confirms inclusion. On an **excluded** trial, Agree confirms exclusion; Disagree means it should have stayed in the group.

Some included-diabetes categories can also ask a separate, required question:

**“Does the registry text state that the trial studies type 2 diabetes?”**

- Type 2 diabetes is stated
- Type 2 diabetes is not stated
- Cannot tell from this packet

If shown, answer it alongside any membership answer and whenever you revise. This describes what the text states; it does not replace the membership judgment, relabel the trial or receive a tie-breaker decision. Finding stated type 2 on these already-included, unresolved-type categories is not itself a reason to disagree. Other diabetes categories retain their valid type-specific disagreement reasons.

### Neoplasm inclusion check

The original sorting removed a trial **only when all its neoplasm headings were on the list of cysts, hamartomas or precancerous conditions**. This is narrower than excluding every nonmalignant condition.

| Displayed classification              | Why the original sorting produced it                                                               | What you judge                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Included in the Neoplasms group**   | At least one neoplasm heading was outside that exclusion list. This does not establish malignancy. | Does the trial study a neoplasm of any behavior?                                                                               |
| **Excluded from the Neoplasms group** | Every neoplasm heading was on the cyst, hamartoma or precancerous-condition list.                  | Is exclusion appropriate? Agree if the trial studies only those conditions and no neoplasm. Disagree if it studies a neoplasm. |

**The list is not the clinical answer.** If you judge a listed condition to be a neoplasm, explain your disagreement with exclusion. This includes a listed condition that you judge clinically to be a neoplasm. A malignant neoplasm and a nonmalignant neoplasm can each justify disagreement with exclusion; choose the corresponding reason. Benign behavior alone does not justify disagreement with inclusion.

## 6. Explain disagreement briefly

The menu shows reasons relevant to the particular question. Choose the closest one and write a short note connecting it to the trial evidence.

| Reason                                   | Meaning                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Not the condition studied**            | The displayed condition is not what the trial investigates.                                                   |
| **MeSH placement is misleading**         | The heading’s position in the hierarchy produced a category that does not fit the clinical condition studied. |
| **Cancer origin, spread or course only** | The extra area follows only from the cancer itself, without a separate indication in that area.               |
| **Excluded by the review policy**        | A specific definition in this guide excludes the assignment. Name that definition in your note.               |
| **Another reason — explain in the note** | None of the listed reasons fits.                                                                              |

Inclusion/exclusion checks also offer specific reasons such as **“Type 1 diabetes only,” “Type 2 diabetes studied,” “Not a neoplasm,”** or **“Nonmalignant neoplasm studied.”** These explain why you disagree with the displayed inclusion or exclusion. They are not instructions to change the underlying trial label.

## 7. How differences are resolved

After both reviewers finish, independent review closes and the investigator authorizes tie-breaking. The adjudicator then sees the displayed assignment and the two recorded answers, notes and uncertainty flags for a unit that needs resolution.

- Agree versus Disagree, or exactly one Cannot tell, requires a tie-breaker.
- Two identical membership answers become the reference answer without a tie-breaker. Two Cannot tell answers remain Cannot tell.
- The adjudicator records Agree or Disagree, identifies which reviewer’s answer is adopted or chooses neither, or leaves the unit unresolved.
- The separate diabetes stated-type answer is not adjudicated.

The adjudicator does not see AI assessments. Reviewers do not see one another’s answers during independent review.

## 8. Read-only examples and what the results mean

Use the separate reference section to return to the teaching examples at any time. No clinical-review answer is entered on those pages.

The three worked examples illustrate:

1. A Metabolic assignment supported by a type 2 diabetes heading: both reviewers agree.
2. A Cardiovascular assignment resulting from a neoplasm heading’s placement: both reviewers disagree because the placement is misleading.
3. A Respiratory assignment from a lung-cancer heading: the reviewers differ; the tie-breaker adopts the disagreement under the cancer-origin policy.

The worked examples are separate from the synthetic practice queue and the 100 formal review units. None enters a study denominator.

The result is **clinician confirmation of displayed assignments**. It is not an independent blinded estimate of classifier accuracy, sensitivity or missed assignments. Area and condition-group results stay separate; the four inclusion/exclusion checks are reported as counts. Missing, Cannot tell and unresolved answers remain distinguishable. The statistical plan determines the reported denominators.

Any separately recorded diabetes stated-type answers are summarized as counts by reviewer, first and last answer, and membership answer. They do not establish an adjudicated diabetes subtype. The Depression exception remains a stated limitation; the study’s separately specified MeSH-only sensitivity analysis is unchanged.
