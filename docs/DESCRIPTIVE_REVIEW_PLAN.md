# Descriptive results from the reviewed sample

Plan identifier: `mu84-descriptive-agreement-1`.

The investigator's October 27 scope decision defers population estimation. This plan describes
the reviewed mappings; it does not estimate performance across the wider trial population or
validate a prevalence benchmark. It changes no clinical question, category, assignment or packet.

## Answers used

Each reviewer saves immutable revisions, then finishes and locks their complete set of 100
answers. The last saved answer at that finish is their independent judgment. Both reviewers
must finish before the investigator closes review and records its snapshot. Disagreements and
every Cannot tell, including two Cannot tells, go to adjudication. The original answers and
the both-Cannot-tell flag are retained after adjudication. An unresolved adjudication remains
unresolved. The separate diabetes stated-type observation is never adjudicated.

## Descriptive outputs

- Report therapeutic-area mappings, condition-row mappings and subgroup checks separately.
  Do not combine their confirmation rates into one primary result.
- For each family, give the number confirmed, not confirmed, unresolved and missing. For AREA
  and ROW, show each count as a percentage of all mappings in that family, with the denominator
  printed. An unresolved mapping is its own outcome, not a negative clinical judgment.
- For AREA and ROW, show the final independent three-by-three answer table, exact agreement
  (matching final answers divided by paired final answers), and unweighted Cohen's kappa across
  Agree, Disagree and Cannot tell. These describe this sample only. Matching Cannot tell answers
  count as reviewer agreement but do not constitute clinical confirmation and still go to
  adjudication. Kappa is unavailable if its chance-agreement denominator is zero.
- For individual categories and subgroup checks, report counts only. Retain the separate
  both-Cannot-tell count, numbers routed to adjudication, unresolved outcomes, revision counts
  and first/final diabetes observations. Do not convert unstated diabetes type into type 2.
- Use only the locked, registered formal export for study results. Synthetic practice and
  training remain excluded. Missing or unfinished records cannot silently become disagreements.

## Deferred analyses

No sampling weights, confidence intervals, population estimates or sensitivity analyses are
part of this initial report. Preserve nominal probabilities, the bound Monte Carlo source and
all provenance for a possible later analysis. Any later population analysis needs its own dated
method decision and must be described as additional analysis, not a prespecified primary result.

Recording this requirement does not approve a gate or open review. The investigator must approve
the scoped requirement, accept the pilot and separately authorize the formal opening.
