# Application scientific contract

This is a sanitized engineering summary. The private source contract and research handoff are not repository content. Direct investigator instructions govern where source documents disagree.

Tier 0 is the deterministic classification. Tier 1 is a versioned provisional AI-curated classification. Tier 2 is an independent clinical assessment followed by separate adjudication. Reviewer agreement and classifier performance are different outcomes; AI-stage consistency is not clinical validity.

Reviewers independently choose INCLUDE, EXCLUDE, CONTEXT_DEPENDENT or INSUFFICIENT_INFORMATION. Every final answer requires rationale and uncertainty; a context-dependent answer requires structured context. Unknown and not-applicable remain distinct. Trial cases can require a descriptor-level decision locked before trial context opens.

Two reviewers assess the same cases independently. The adjudicator is a separate role, reads human judgments only after the campaign closes and adjudication is authorized, and cannot access classifier predictions. Each required level must have two initial submissions; single-reviewed adjudication is not enabled. Final responses are locked. An amendment must preserve the original response, identify the reason and authorization, and cannot rewrite a locked reference. The pre-context Step 1 in a trial case cannot be amended after submission, because submission immediately makes trial context available. This restriction does not prevent documented amendments to descriptor-only or trial-level judgments while review remains open.

The five record classes are development, synthetic pilot, rubric calibration, held-out validation and adjudication. No reclassification can turn a development or pilot record into validation. Formal activation must verify provenance completeness and uniqueness; exclusion ledgers; sample/frame/ledger versions, strata, inclusion probabilities, seed and source; approved scientific choices; immutable content hashes; and assigned independent roles. Frozen material is versioned, never overwritten.

Tier version, sample, rubric, adjudicator, analysis plan and formal authorization remain pending. The known coverage limitation for classifier-negative / curated-positive cases is an approval warning, not a problem solved by the application. Sample sizes and strata are not scientific defaults embedded in the application.

The ten fictional SYN- pilot cases are interface tests and never enter performance metrics. Separate explicitly synthetic test bundles exercise analytical code. No displayed engineering result is a clinical finding. CONTEXT_DEPENDENT and INSUFFICIENT_INFORMATION handling in real analysis awaits an approved frozen plan.

Module B is optional, unapproved and separate, with its own cases, rubric, sampling, outcomes, permissions and export.
